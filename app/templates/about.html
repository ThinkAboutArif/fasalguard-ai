About FasalGuard AI

An intelligent crop disease detection system that helps farmers and agricultural experts identify plant diseases instantly using deep learning — no agricultural expertise required.

What is FasalGuard?

FasalGuard (from Urdu/Hindi fasal meaning "crop") is a web-based AI application designed to solve one of Pakistan's biggest agricultural challenges: early and accurate disease detection.

Every year, crop diseases destroy up to 40% of global food production. In Pakistan, where agriculture employs nearly 40% of the workforce and contributes 24% to GDP, late disease identification leads to massive yield losses, wasted pesticides, and food insecurity.

FasalGuard bridges this gap by putting a plant pathologist in every farmer's pocket. Simply upload a photo of a diseased leaf, and the AI tells you exactly what the disease is, how severe it is, and what to do about it — in plain language, with specific chemical names and dosages.

How It Works

1

Upload

Take a clear photo of the affected leaf and upload it through the web interface. Works on any phone or laptop.

2

Analyse

Our EfficientNet-B0 deep learning model processes the image in under 2 seconds on a standard laptop CPU.

3

Diagnose

The AI identifies the exact disease from 38 possible conditions across 14 crop types.

4

Treat

Get instant recommendations: what chemical to use, how much to mix, and how to prevent future outbreaks.

The Technology

EfficientNet-B0

A state-of-the-art convolutional neural network pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on 53,114 crop leaf images from the PlantVillage dataset.

99.17% Accuracy

Validated on 5,297 held-out images. The model correctly identifies diseases with near-perfect accuracy on clean leaf photographs.

Temperature Scaling

Advanced calibration technique ensures confidence scores reflect real uncertainty — not artificial overconfidence.

Grad-CAM Ready

Visual heatmap integration (coming post-presentation) will highlight exactly which leaf regions triggered the diagnosis.

The Dataset

We trained FasalGuard on the PlantVillage dataset, the world's largest open-access collection of crop leaf images:

  • 53,114 images after data cleaning (1,191 blurry images removed)
  • 38 disease classes across 14 crop species
  • 80/10/10 split for training, validation, and testing
  • Images are standardized close-up leaf shots with plain backgrounds

Crops covered: Apple, Blueberry, Cherry, Corn (Maize), Grape, Orange, Peach, Pepper, Potato, Raspberry, Soybean, Squash, Strawberry, and Tomato.

Known Limitations

  • Image quality matters: The model performs best on close-up leaf images with plain backgrounds (like PlantVillage). Photos of full plants, fields, or complex backgrounds may produce incorrect results.
  • Not a replacement for experts: FasalGuard is a decision-support tool. For critical or uncertain cases, always consult a certified agricultural extension officer.
  • Regional chemical availability: Recommended chemicals may not be available in all regions. Substitute with locally approved equivalents when necessary.
  • Grad-CAM pending: Visual heatmap overlay is prepared in the backend but not yet integrated into the UI.
