About FasalGuard AI

An intelligent crop disease detection system that helps farmers identify plant diseases instantly using deep learning.

What is FasalGuard?

FasalGuard (from Urdu fasal meaning "crop") is a web-based AI application designed to solve Pakistan's biggest agricultural challenge: early and accurate disease detection.

Every year, crop diseases destroy up to 40% of global food production. In Pakistan, where agriculture employs nearly 40% of the workforce, late disease identification leads to massive yield losses.

FasalGuard bridges this gap by putting a plant pathologist in every farmer's pocket. Upload a photo of a diseased leaf, and the AI tells you exactly what the disease is, how severe it is, and what to do about it.

How It Works

1

Upload

Take a clear photo of the affected leaf and upload it. Works on any phone or laptop.

2

Analyse

Our FasalGuard-B0 model processes the image in under 2 seconds on CPU.

3

Diagnose

The AI identifies the exact disease from 38 possible conditions across 14 crop types.

4

Treat

Get instant recommendations: what chemical to use, how much to mix, and prevention tips.

The Technology

FasalGuard-B0

State-of-the-art CNN based on EfficientNet, fine-tuned on 53,114 crop leaf images from PlantVillage.

99.17% Accuracy

Validated on 5,297 held-out images. Near-perfect accuracy on clean leaf photographs.

Temperature Scaling

Advanced calibration ensures confidence scores reflect real uncertainty.

Grad-CAM

Visual heatmap highlights exactly which leaf regions triggered the diagnosis.

The Dataset

Trained on the PlantVillage dataset, the world's largest open-access collection of crop leaf images:

  • 53,114 images after data cleaning (1,191 blurry images removed)
  • 38 disease classes across 14 crop species
  • 80/10/10 split for training, validation, and testing
  • Standardized close-up leaf shots with plain backgrounds
